## 2) How Scoring Works

- **A–D maturity:** Each answer is scored A=1 (none, ad hoc or person-dependent), B=2 (partial or untested), C=3 (documented, owned, not recently tested) or D=4 (tested in the last 12 months with logs, CAPA and viable alternates).

- **Score:** The move score is the mean of its A–D scores, rounded to the nearest whole number, with exact halves rounded down.
- **Caps:**
  - A move is capped at 2 if its evidence average is below E2.
  - A move is also capped at 2 if its prerequisite question is answered A.
  - A move scores 0 (Unknown) only if more than half of its answers are A at E0.
- **Confidence:**
  - High requires more than half of the answers at E3.
  - Medium requires more than half at E2 or E3 and at least one E3.
  - Everything else is Low.
  - A failed consistency check forces Low.
